Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Snip Poster runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server and does not collect, sell or share your data with anyone.

What it stores, and where

  • TikTok sign-in tokens and your TikTok display name, received when you connect your account. They are stored in a file readable only by your user account on your computer and are used only to upload your videos.
  • Your settings and history: the folders you chose, captions, and which videos were sent and when. Also stored only on your computer.

What it sends to TikTok

Only the video files you have it post, sent directly from your computer to TikTok through TikTok's Content Posting API. TikTok's own privacy policy covers what TikTok does with them.

Removing your data

Press Disconnect in Snip Poster to delete the stored tokens, revoke access in TikTok's settings under Security → Manage app permissions, and delete the Snip Poster folder to remove everything else.
