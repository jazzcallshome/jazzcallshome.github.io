Snip Poster

Snip Poster is a small desktop app for macOS that helps a creator share their own lyric videos to TikTok. It watches a folder on the creator's computer for newly rendered videos, picks times through the day, and uploads each video to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft. The creator then reviews, edits and publishes the post themselves in the TikTok app.

How it uses TikTok

  • The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves the user.info.basic and video.upload permissions.
  • Videos are sent with the Content Posting API in upload (draft) mode. Nothing is published without the creator finishing the post in TikTok.
  • Everything runs on the creator's own computer. There is no Snip Poster server.
